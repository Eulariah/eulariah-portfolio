Sample Work

How I actually respond to customers.

Real response samples across live chat, email, and phone — showing exactly how I acknowledge, resolve, and follow up on every ticket.

Live Chat — Delayed Shopify Order

Customer messages asking where their order is — 3 days past the estimated delivery date.
"Hi Sarah, thanks for reaching out — and sorry for the wait, I know that's frustrating. Let me pull up your order now. I can see order #4821 shipped on the 12th and the carrier shows it's currently held at the local depot. I've flagged it with them directly for priority release. Here's what I'll do: I'll email you the moment it moves, and if there's no update within 24 hours I'll go ahead and send a replacement at no cost. Does that work for you?"
✉

Email — Refund Request

Customer emails for a refund on a damaged item received two weeks ago — outside the usual 7-day window.
Subject: Re: Damaged item — Order #7743 Hi James, Thank you for sending photos of the damage — I can see this clearly wasn't right, and I'm sorry it arrived that way. While this is just outside our standard 7-day window, the item was faulty on arrival, so I've approved a full refund as an exception. You'll see it back on your card within 3–5 business days, and I've sent a confirmation to this email. Thanks for your patience — please reach out directly if anything else comes up, I'll take care of it. Warm regards, Eulariah
☎

Phone — Billing Dispute Escalation

Customer calls in upset about being charged twice for a subscription renewal.
"Thank you for holding, this is Eulariah — I understand you were charged twice this month, and I want to get that sorted for you right away. I can confirm two charges on the 3rd, and the second one shouldn't be there. I'm reversing that duplicate now — you'll see the refund within 5 business days, and I'll send a written confirmation to your email before we hang up. I've also added a note to your account so our billing team can double-check next month's renewal doesn't repeat this. Is there anything else I can help you with today?"
